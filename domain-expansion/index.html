ABOUT THE EVENT

Domain Expansion is an online AI Prompt Challenge designed to test participants' creativity, speed, understanding of domains, and ability to construct effective AI prompts.

The challenge consists of two rounds. Participants work with different domains to generate images or create prompts according to the strict requirements set by the organizers.

THE CHALLENGE

Two phases to prove your AI mastery

ROUND 01

AI IMAGE GENERATION CHALLENGE

  • Each participant is assigned one domain from the available list.
  • Use the assigned domain as the base theme.
  • Generate a photo/image or poster using an AI tool.
  • Time limit: 5 minutes.
  • Fastest completion earns 1 point.
  • Use "Raise Hand" feature in Google Meet upon completion.
  • Selected participants will present their generated image.
  • Judges review based on requirements, validity, and creativity.

ROUND 02

AI PROMPT CREATION CHALLENGE

  • Participants are given a poster requirement description (theme, style, details, conditions).
  • Create an effective, detailed AI prompt that can generate the required poster.
  • No visual sample will be provided; rely solely on the textual description.
  • Speed and prompt quality are critical for evaluation.
