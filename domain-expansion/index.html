WHAT IS DOMAIN EXPANSION?

Domain Expansion is an online AI Prompt Challenge designed to test participants’ creativity, speed, understanding of domains, and ability to construct effective AI prompts.

The challenge consists of two competitive rounds. Participants will work with different domains and use generative AI tools to create visual assets or construct precision prompts according to real-time criteria.

// SYSTEM STAGES

THE CHALLENGE

Two high-octane rounds testing prompt crafting speed, creativity, and accuracy.

01
// STAGE 01

ROUND 01: AI IMAGE GENERATION

05:00 LIMIT

Participants will be assigned one domain from the three available domains. They must use the assigned domain to generate a photo/image or poster using an AI tool.

SPEED POINT

The participant who completes the task first earns 1 point.

SUBMISSION PROTOCOL

The host will call participants one by one to present the generated image. Judges will review submitted images based on challenge requirements and validity/creativity of output.

02
// STAGE 02

ROUND 02: AI PROMPT CREATION

ADVANCED SYNTHESIS

Participants will be given a poster requirement rather than a sample poster. Organizers will describe the required theme, style, details, and conditions.

Participants must create an effective AI prompt that can generate the required poster. The best and fastest prompt will be declared the winner of the round.
