THE CHALLENGE

Domain Expansion is an online AI Prompt Challenge designed to test participants' creativity, speed, understanding of domains, and ability to construct effective AI prompts.

The challenge consists of two rounds. Participants will work with different domains and use AI tools to generate images or create prompts according to the given requirements.

AI IMAGE

05:00MINUTES
TIME LIMIT — 5 MINUTES
  • Participants are assigned one domain from the three available domains.
  • They must use the assigned domain to generate a photo or poster image using an AI tool.
  • The participant who completes the task first earns 1 point.
  • After completing the task, participants must use the Raise Hand feature in Google Meet.
  • The host calls participants one by one to present their generated image.
  • Judges review submissions based on requirements and the validity and creativity of the output.

AI PROMPT
CREATION

  • Participants receive a poster requirement rather than a sample poster.
  • The organizers describe the required theme, style, details, and conditions.
  • Participants create an effective AI prompt that can generate the required poster.
  • The best and fastest prompt will be considered the winner.
